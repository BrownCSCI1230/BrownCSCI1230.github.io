Background

To prevent aliasing from textures in our scenes, we need to know how much of an object's texture is covered by a single pixel. In other words: if we look through the quadrilateral region of the view plane that corresponds to one pixel, and a textured object is visible, what region of that object's texture map will we see?

That region is itself a quadrilateral in texture space (bottom right of Figure 1), and the overall goal of this algo is to compute the lengths of its sides.
